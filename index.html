Keep your runs safe

An account puts your runs on any device you sign in from, and gets them back if you lose the phone. Everything works without one — your runs simply stay here.

What an account stores. Your email, every run you record including its GPS track, any routes you save, and whatever profile details you choose to give.

Where it goes. A Google Firebase project in the United States, owned by the person who shared this app with you. Security rules stop other users reading your data; the project's owner is technically able to.

A GPS track shows where you live if you run from home. The privacy zone in Settings trims the ends of a route before you share it, but the full track is what gets stored.

Nothing is sold. No advertising, no analytics. You can delete all of it from Settings, and without an account nothing leaves this phone at all.

About & privacy

What this app stores, and what it isn't

Not medical advice. Training plans here are generated by software. They don't know your health or your injuries. Running carries a risk of injury — check with a doctor before starting, stop if something hurts, and treat the plan as a suggestion. You use it at your own risk.

On this phone. Runs, routes, your profile and settings are stored in this browser. Clearing website data, or deleting the app from your home screen, deletes them. Keep a backup file if they matter.

With an account. Runs, routes, profile and plan are also held in a Google Firebase project in the United States, owned by the person who shared this app. Rules stop other users reading your data; the owner is technically able to.

Who else sees anything. Map tiles come from OpenStreetMap, so their servers see roughly where you are while a map is open. Snapping a run to streets sends the area you ran — not the run — to the Overpass service; that can be turned off above. Strava sees your location only if you connect it yourself.

Not for navigation or emergencies. GPS is approximate, especially between tall buildings, and the app can be wrong about where you are. Distances and any calorie figure are estimates.

No warranty. This is a personal project given away free. It can break, and data can be lost. Not intended for under-16s.

Sync across devices

Ready

Optional. Runs stay on this phone and work offline either way; signing in also keeps them on any other device you sign in from. Runs and routes sync — settings stay per-device.

Backup

One file holding every run and saved route, to keep somewhere safe or move to another phone. Restoring adds anything missing and leaves what you already have alone. Your Strava keys are deliberately left out.

Bring runs in

Adds a run recorded somewhere else — an Apple Watch workout exported from Strava, say — to your history here.
